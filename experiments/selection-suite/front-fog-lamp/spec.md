# spec: Front Fog Lamp (LED)

## Product Type
Front fog lamp for a passenger car, mounted in the lower front fascia, one lamp per side.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car.

## Function
- Emits a white beam that improves road illumination in fog, snow, heavy rain or dust
- Switched on by the driver independently of the passing beam

## Main Parts
- Polycarbonate outer lens with hard coating
- Reflector and projection optics
- LED module (non-replaceable, soldered on a circuit board)
- Electronic LED driver (constant-current regulator) on the same board
- Die-cast aluminium heat sink, plastic housing with pressure-equalisation membrane
- Connector to the vehicle 12 V harness, mounting bracket with vertical aim adjuster

## Technology
LED light source with electronic driver; 12 V supply; IP67 sealed housing.
