# spec: Replacement Driver Airbag Module with Steering Wheel

## Product Type
Aftermarket replacement steering wheel with an integrated driver airbag module, sold to replace the original steering
wheel of passenger cars.

## Vehicle Category
M1 and N1 vehicles.

## Function
- Restrains the driver's head and chest in a severe frontal impact, in addition to the safety belt
- Steering control of the vehicle (rim, spokes, hub)

## Main Parts
- Steering wheel rim and hub with spline connection to the steering column
- Airbag cushion (nylon fabric) folded in the hub
- Pyrotechnic gas generator with electrical igniter
- Airbag cover with tear seam and horn switch
- Clock spring connection to the vehicle airbag control unit

## Technology
Pyrotechnic inflator triggered by the vehicle's existing airbag control unit.
