# spec: Front Radar Sensor for Emergency Braking

## Product Type
Long-range front radar sensor that is the main sensor of the emergency braking function of a passenger car.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car (also offered for N1 light commercial vehicles).

## Function
- Detects vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists ahead of the vehicle
- Warns the driver of an imminent forward collision (optical and acoustic warning)
- Requests braking from the brake control unit to avoid or mitigate a collision
- Monitors itself and reports failures to the driver

## Main Parts
- 77 GHz radar transceiver with antenna array
- Signal-processing microcontroller with collision-avoidance software
- Radome (plastic cover) behind the front emblem
- Mounting bracket with alignment mechanism
- Connector for CAN-FD communication and 12 V supply

## Technology
Electronic sensor module with embedded software; communicates with the brake ECU and the instrument cluster.
