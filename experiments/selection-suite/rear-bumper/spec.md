# spec: Rear Bumper Assembly

## Product Type
Rear bumper assembly of a passenger car: painted fascia, energy absorber, steel cross-member and integrated
retro-reflectors and parking sensors.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car.

## Function
- Protects the vehicle rear in low-speed collisions (energy absorption, damage limitation)
- Makes the vehicle visible to other road users at night with two red rear retro-reflectors
- Detects obstacles behind the vehicle when reversing (parking assistance)
- Exterior styling of the rear end, cut-out for the tailpipe

## Main Parts
- Bumper fascia (painted polypropylene)
- Steel bumper beam (cross-member) with crash boxes
- Energy absorber (EPP foam)
- Two red retro-reflectors (Class IA) clipped into the fascia
- Four ultrasonic parking sensors with wiring harness
- Lower diffuser and tow-eye cover

## Technology
Injection-moulded plastics, high-strength steel beam, ultrasonic sensors connected to the parking-assistance ECU (12 V).
