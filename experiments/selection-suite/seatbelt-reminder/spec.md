# spec: Safety-Belt Reminder System

## Product Type
Safety-belt reminder system for all seating positions of a passenger car.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car with five seats.

## Function
- Detects whether each seating position is occupied and whether its belt is buckled
- Warns the driver optically and acoustically when a belt of an occupied seat is not fastened
- Escalates the warning with vehicle speed and time

## Main Parts
- Occupancy sensor mats in the front seats
- Buckle switches in all belt buckles
- Evaluation function in the body control unit
- Tell-tales in the instrument cluster and overhead console, acoustic signal

## Technology
Sensors and electronic evaluation on the 12 V vehicle electrical system.
