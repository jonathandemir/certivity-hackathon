# spec: Replacement Exhaust Silencer for Motorcycles

## Product Type
Non-original replacement rear silencer (slip-on) sold in the aftermarket for a 650 cc road motorcycle.

## Vehicle Category
L3 — motorcycle.

## Function
- Reduces the exhaust noise of the motorcycle
- Replaces the original rear silencer without changes to the catalytic converter or engine settings

## Main Parts
- Stainless-steel inlet pipe and link pipe with clamp
- Titanium silencer body with sound-absorbing glass-fibre packing
- Perforated inner tube and removable end cap
- Mounting bracket and heat shield

## Technology
Absorptive silencer; markings with manufacturer name and type on the silencer body.
