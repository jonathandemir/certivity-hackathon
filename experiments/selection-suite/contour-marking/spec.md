# spec: Retro-Reflective Contour Marking Tape

## Product Type
Retro-reflective marking tape applied as full contour marking along the sides and rear of semi-trailers.

## Vehicle Category
O4 — semi-trailer.

## Function
- Makes the length and width of the trailer visible at night when illuminated by headlamps
- White marking on the sides, red (or yellow) marking at the rear

## Main Parts
- Microprismatic retro-reflective film, 50 mm wide, in white, red and yellow
- Pressure-sensitive adhesive with liner
- Approval marking printed into the film at regular intervals

## Technology
Microprismatic retro-reflective sheeting; applied by the trailer manufacturer to the body panels.
