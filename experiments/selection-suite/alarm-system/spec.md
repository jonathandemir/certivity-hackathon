# spec: Vehicle Alarm System

## Product Type
Vehicle alarm system that is permanently fitted by the vehicle manufacturer to passenger cars.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car.

## Function
- Detects opening of doors, bonnet and boot, and intrusion into the passenger compartment
- Gives an acoustic alarm (siren) and an optical alarm (flashing of the direction indicators)
- Is set and unset together with the central locking via the vehicle key
- Does not immobilise the engine (separate system, not part of this product)

## Main Parts
- Alarm control unit
- Battery-backed siren
- Ultrasonic interior-movement sensor in the roof console
- Door, bonnet and boot contact switches
- Status LED on the dashboard

## Technology
Electronic control unit and sensors on the 12 V vehicle electrical system; communication with the body controller via CAN.
