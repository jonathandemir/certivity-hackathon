# spec: Windscreen

## Product Type
Laminated windscreen of a passenger car.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car.

## Function
- Provides the driver's forward field of view
- Protects the occupants against wind, weather and objects
- Holds the interior rear-view mirror base and is bonded into the body opening

## Main Parts
- Two layers of 2.1 mm float glass with a 0.76 mm PVB interlayer
- Black ceramic print band along the edge
- Mirror base bonded to the inner surface
- Polyurethane bonding bead and plastic edge trim

## Technology
Laminated safety glass, no heating wires, antennas or other electrical functions.
