# spec: Electric Power Steering Column

## Product Type
Column-type electric power steering (EPS) for passenger cars.

## Vehicle Category
M1 — passenger car.

## Function
- Transmits the driver's steering input from the steering wheel to the steering gear
- Provides electric steering assistance depending on vehicle speed and steering torque
- Keeps a mechanical connection between steering wheel and road wheels if the assistance fails
- Absorbs energy in a frontal impact (collapsible column)

## Main Parts
- Collapsible steering column with adjustment for reach and rake
- Torque sensor and steering-angle sensor
- Brushless electric motor with worm gear
- Electronic control unit with control software
- Intermediate shaft with universal joints

## Technology
Electromechanical actuator with ECU on the 12 V vehicle electrical system, CAN communication; no automated steering functions.
