# spec: Mechanical coupling device, RINGFEDER type 86/G110 (VBG Group Truck Equipment)

## Product Type
Mechanical coupling device between a towing vehicle and a trailer (miscellaneous coupling device, class S).

## Function
- Connects towing vehicle and trailer and transmits the coupling forces

## Main Parts
- Coupling body (type 86/G110)

## Technology
Mechanical device; D-value 66 kN, Dc-value 35 kN, static vertical load S 500 kg, V-value 15.5 kN.
