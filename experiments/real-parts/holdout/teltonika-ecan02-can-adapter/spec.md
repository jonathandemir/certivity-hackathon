# spec: Contactless CAN bus adapter, Teltonika ECAN02

## Product Type
Contactless adapter that reads vehicle CAN bus data through the insulation of the CAN wires and forwards the signals to a tracking device.

## Function
- Reads CAN High/CAN Low signals without damaging the wires
- Forwards CAN data to a tracker

## Main Parts
- Housing with slots for the CAN wires, double-sided adhesive tape for mounting
- Electronics, powered from the on-board supply (10–30 V DC)

## Technology
Electronic sub-assembly installed on the vehicle wiring.
