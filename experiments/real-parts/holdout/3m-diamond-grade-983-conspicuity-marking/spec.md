# spec: Retro-reflective conspicuity marking tape, 3M Diamond Grade Series 983 (983-10, -71, -72, -21)

## Product Type
Retro-reflective conspicuity marking tape for large vehicles: sides and rear of trucks, front, sides and rear of trailers.

## Function
- Increases the conspicuity of a vehicle when viewed from the side or rear

## Main Parts
- Prismatic lenses in a transparent synthetic resin, sealed
- Pressure-sensitive adhesive with clear polymeric liner, pre-sealed edges, non-metallic construction
- Colours: white, yellow, red, fluorescent yellow

## Technology
Passive retro-reflective film, no electrical parts.
