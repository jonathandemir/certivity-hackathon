# spec: LED fog lamp with cornering light, HELLA SlimLine, part no. 1NL 016 340-001

## Product Type
Combined front fog lamp and cornering light in one slim rectangular module, for both vehicle sides.

## Function
- Front fog light
- Cornering light

## Main Parts
- LED light source
- Housing with carrier frame, installable from the back and front
- HB3 electrical connector
- Vertical adjustment (-4° to +4°)

## Technology
LED; multi-voltage 9–32 V (rated 12 V and 24 V); 5 W at 13.5 V, 16 W at 28 V; weight 560.5 g; IP 5K4K and IP 5K9K; operating temperature -40 °C to +60 °C.
