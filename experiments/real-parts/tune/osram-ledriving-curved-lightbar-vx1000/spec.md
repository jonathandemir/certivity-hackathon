# spec: LED driving light (curved light bar), OSRAM LEDriving Curved Lightbar VX1000-CB SR SM, order reference LEDDL135-CB SR

## Product Type
Curved LED light bar as additional high beam (driving light), for central single mounting.

## Function
- Driving light, additional high beam, combo beam for far-field and near-field illumination
- Additional boost mode and amber position light (manufacturer: these two functions must not be used on public roads)

## Main Parts
- LED light bar, 1109 x 67 x 35 mm, 3100 g
- Brackets for mounting on bumper, grille or windshield
- Open-wire connection

## Technology
LED; 108 W nominal (test voltage 13.2 V).
