# spec: Vehicle telematics tracker, Teltonika FMB150

## Product Type
GNSS/GSM/Bluetooth vehicle tracker with integrated CAN chip.

## Function
- Vehicle position tracking (GNSS) and data transmission (GSM)
- Reading vehicle data via integrated CAN chip; Bluetooth

## Main Parts
- Electronics with GNSS and GSM modules, internal GNSS/GSM antennas
- Integrated CAN chip
- Internal battery

## Technology
Electronic sub-assembly connected to the vehicle.
