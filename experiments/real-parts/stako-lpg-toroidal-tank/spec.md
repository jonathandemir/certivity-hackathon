# spec: LPG fuel container, internal toroidal, STAKO Legacy T12 type ZT-271.00H

## Product Type
LPG fuel tank, internal toroidal type, for an LPG-powered vehicle.

## Function
- Stores liquefied petroleum gas for the vehicle's engine

## Main Parts
- Toroidal steel container, nominal outer diameter 520 mm, nominal height 225 mm
- Water capacity 36 l, usable LPG capacity approx. 29 l
- Flange for an Italian-type multivalve (head fixed with six screws); only approved multivalves listed on the tank certificate may be used

## Technology
Pressure vessel for LPG.
