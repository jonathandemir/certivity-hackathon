# spec: Front Bumper

## Product Type

Front bumper assembly — exterior front-end module of a passenger car combining the bumper fascia, energy absorption,
integrated front fog lamps and driver-assistance sensors.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car.

## Function

- Low-speed impact protection of the vehicle front (energy absorption)
- Pedestrian protection in frontal impacts (lower leg contact zone)
- Exterior styling and aerodynamics of the front end
- Mounting of front fog lamps and their illumination function
- Housing for parking-assistance ultrasonic sensors and a front radar sensor
- Front registration plate mounting

## Main Parts

- Bumper fascia (painted outer cover)
- Bumper beam (crash cross-member)
- Energy absorber (foam)
- Lower grille and air inlets
- Front fog lamps (LED) with bezels
- Ultrasonic parking sensors (4x) with brackets
- Front radar sensor mount
- Towing eye cover
- Front registration plate holder
- Mounting brackets and clips
